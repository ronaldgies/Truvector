About Us

Built by people who respect the trade.

Truvector Industries was formed on a simple idea: the best small manufacturers in the Pacific Northwest deserve a long-term home that protects what they built and gives it room to grow.

We invest in shops with skilled teams and real craft. We keep the names, the people and the standards that made them worth investing in, and we add what a single shop rarely has on its own: shared engineering depth, purchasing strength, and a wider horizon.

If you own a manufacturing business and are thinking about what comes next, we would like to hear from you, in confidence.
